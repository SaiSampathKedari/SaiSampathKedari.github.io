Research

When More Data Cannot Separate Two Parameters Course project 2024, rebuilt 2025

In an SIR epidemic model the infection rate enters only as a product γκ, so the Fisher information for (γ, κ) has rank one and no amount of data can separate the two parameters. The posterior spreads along a curve with two mirror-image branches. A Laplace approximation replaces the curve with a straight line, and an adaptive sampler follows the curve but stays on one branch.
